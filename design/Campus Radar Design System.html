Campus Radar · Design system v0.2 · Working name

Quiet surfaces. Clear answers.

A calm, modern product language for finding opportunities that fit a student. Soft canvas, big rounded surfaces, one neutral typeface and almost no color, so the deadline and the proof of trust are the things you notice.

Relevant

Filtered by degree, year, location, eligibility and experience.

Trustworthy

Source link, eligibility, deadline and last-verified date on every card.

Never lose track

Saved items, application status and deadline reminders.

What changed from v0.1: no mono labels or hairline boxes, no blue accent. Large radii, pill controls, a single typeface and a neutral navy primary, based on a close read of Contra. All listings and organisations here are made-up sample data.

Foundations

Color

Cool greys with a faint blue lean, a navy primary and almost nothing else. Click a swatch to copy its value. The button at the bottom of the index switches the whole page.

Contrast, live

Computed from the active theme's tokens. Text needs 4.5 or higher.

Rules

Navy is the action color

Primary buttons and the Create button use it. In dark mode it flips to near-white, so a primary action is always the highest-contrast thing on screen.

Violet is for "this fits you"

Only the fit line and match moments use it. If violet appears anywhere else it stops meaning anything.

Green, amber and red are listing state

Verified, closing within 7 days, error. Always with an icon or words, never color alone.

No borders on top of borders

Use a fill step (canvas, panel, muted) before reaching for a line. One hairline per surface.

Rules

Trust and voice

Trust is the product, so the interface enforces it. Write like a well-informed senior would: plain, specific, with the date in the sentence.

Last verified Badge Behaviour
0 to 14 days Verified 3 days ago Shown normally, ranked by deadline.
15 to 30 days Re-checking · 19 days Shown, ranked lower, queued for re-check.
Over 30 days Not recently checked Hidden from default results. Still opens from a saved list.

Starting windows. Tune them to how often your sources really change. The badge is computed from the date and cannot be set by hand.

Required on every listing

Source link. No source, no listing.
Who can apply. Degree, year, skills, experience.
Deadline. Or "Rolling" with a re-check date.
Last verified. A person, a date.
Mode and location. Remote, hybrid, on site, city.

Say it like this

DoCloses 2 Nov, 6 days left

Dates read "2 Nov". Money reads ₹25,000 / month. No exclamation marks. Buttons start with a verb.

Mentor notes

Straight talk

  1. Why v0.1 felt generic.

    It used the standard recipe: mono labels, hairline boxes, 8px corners, a blue accent. Nothing in it said "product". The new version gets its personality from three things only: large radii, pill controls, and restraint with color.

  2. I could not get the exact font.

    Contra's public site uses GT Standard, which is licensed. Geist is the closest free option. I read the in-app weights off your screenshots, so confirm them against the real app in your browser's inspector if you need them exact.

  3. Do not copy their imagery strategy.

    Contra's cards lean on photography and portfolios. Your listings will be text. That is why I added monogram tiles and the dark spotlight card. Without them, big radii on text-only cards look empty.

  4. Do not copy their name, wordmark or logo.

    This system borrows proportions and patterns, not identity. Campus Radar gets its own mark, and you should keep it that way.

  5. Build five things first.

    Opportunity card, search card, pill filter, verified badge and the sidebar. Test with five students before touching anything else.
